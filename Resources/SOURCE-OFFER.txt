the GNU General Public License, version 2 or later, plus LAME (LGPL) and libwebp and dav1d (BSD). Their licences are
in this folder, and THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt lists each part and where its source comes from.

Written offer for the engine's source code: for at least three years from the date of this release, Snappy Crew will
give anyone who asks a copy of the complete corresponding source code for this version of the media engine (FFmpeg,
x264, x265 and the build script), for no more than the cost of sending it. To ask, contact Snappy Crew through
https://snappycrew.com.
